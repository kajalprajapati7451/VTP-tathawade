About VTP Tathawade

Contemporary Homes in a Thriving Location

VTP Project Tathawade Pune introduces a thoughtfully designed residential community that blends modern lifestyle with everyday convenience. Situated in the fast-developing area of Tathawade, this project is ideal for those seeking a well-connected yet peaceful living environment.

This residential development is crafted to meet the expectations of today's homebuyers by offering smartly planned 1, 2, and 3 BHK apartments. Each residence is designed with attention to detail, ensuring efficient use of space, cross ventilation, and ample natural light. The project focuses on providing a comfortable and practical lifestyle supported by modern infrastructure.

Being a part of a well-planned township by VTP Group, it offers a combination of quality construction, contemporary design, and long-term value for both homeowners and investors.
